Our Story

Namaste, Peoria.

Indian Tadka started as a product of our imagination — an Indian restaurant with modern ambience, a pleasant bar, and genuinely excellent service, all in one place. It's a combination we rarely saw, so we set out to build it ourselves.

Everything on the table starts the same way it does in an Indian home: with tadka, the sizzle of whole spices hitting hot oil, the sound that means dinner is about to happen. We cook with fresh ingredients and Halal-certified meats, and our flavors run from mellow to fiery — every one of them built to pack a punch.

— The Indian Tadka Kitchen

Tandoor-Fired

Naan, tikka, and tandoori chicken cooked the traditional way — in a real clay tandoor, every single day.

Dum-Style Biryani

Rice and meat sealed together and slow-steamed the Hyderabad way — never pre-mixed, never rushed.

Spice, Your Way

Mild for the curious, fiery for the regulars. Tell us your heat level and we'll tune the tadka to match.
